# Support

**Product questions** (what something means, how to do something): start at [docs.bitcoinuniverse.io](https://docs.bitcoinuniverse.io). If the documentation does not answer you, that is a documentation bug; open an issue on the matching `docs-*` repository saying what you were trying to learn.

**Availability questions** (is something down, why is a flow gated): the status pages under [docs.bitcoinuniverse.io](https://docs.bitcoinuniverse.io) are the authority, and the live endpoints they name let you check yourself.

**Developer questions** (APIs, SDKs, protocol rules): each product's developer section links the API reference, schemas, and examples. If a contract and its documentation disagree, open an issue; the contract wins until the fix lands.

**Lost or stuck assets**: read the recovery page of the product you used before anything else. Chain-level outcomes cannot be reversed by support, and nobody who claims otherwise is telling the truth.

**Security problems**: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), privately.
